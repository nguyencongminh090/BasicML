early downsampling more room, or keep it native 28x28 like the sibling
demos.

*shape* — 5 conv layers, channel pattern that grows then plateaus, pooling
only after conv1/conv2/conv5, 2 Dropout-regularized FC layers, ReLU
throughout — but with 3x3/stride-1 convs and smaller channel counts) and
native 28x28 input (no upscaling).
