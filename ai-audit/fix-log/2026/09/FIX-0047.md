## Decision

- Did not re-tune and re-run Muon after observing its divergence (see
  Conclusion) -- reported the result honestly instead. `MUON_LR=0.02` was
  reused unchanged from `train_optimizer_comparison.py`'s much smaller
  32-hidden-unit MLP problem; the divergence shape (stable through ~epoch 7,
  then loss climbing every epoch after, no LR decay anywhere in the script)
  is the standard signature of a learning rate too high for this parameter
  scale, not evidence against Muon as a method -- flagged as the likely fix
  rather than silently attempting more configurations unprompted.
