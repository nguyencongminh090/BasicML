## Description

Add an animated demo contrasting mini-batch gradient descent with full-batch
(BatchGD) for linear regression. At each epoch, freeze the current `(w, b)` and
draw the full-batch gradient as one bold descent arrow plus a thin fan of the
per-mini-batch gradient directions from the same point, over the MSE cost
contour. Trace two optimizer paths on the surface: mini-batch SGD and mini-batch
Momentum, to show momentum stabilising the noisy per-batch direction. Include a
panel quantifying the per-batch angle to the full-batch direction.
